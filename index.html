Who We Are

MY Society aspires to cultivate and develop generations of youth who are grounded in the foundations of our faith and traditions all while aspiring to be leaders worthy of the Imam of our time (AJTF).

Our Pillars

Set out by Marhum Syed Asad Jafri, we provide a platform for youth to succeed in these specific areas:

  • Spiritual Development
  • Academic Development
  • Professional Development
  • Social & Recreational Activities

Our Projects

Initiatives designed to support, educate, and elevate our community.

Camp Asad

An immersive outdoor retreat fostering spiritual growth, brotherhood, and leadership through nature and teamwork.

Scholarships

Financial support and academic recognition for dedicated youth pursuing higher education and excellence.

Tutoring

Peer-led academic support helping students excel in their studies and build confidence in the classroom.

Sports

Organized athletic leagues and events promoting physical health, discipline, and community bonds.
